# Booking and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

## 1. Booking a group class
- You can pay for one class.
- You must log in before you book.
- Some classes are free. A free class also needs a login and the Health Notice, but no payment. You can cancel a free class any time before it starts.
- Before you book, you must accept our Health Notice.
- Your seat is confirmed after payment or after the credit is used. We send an email with the Zoom link. The link is also shown in "My bookings" once your booking is confirmed.
- Classes have limited places. A full class shows "Full".
- If you pay by card, we hold your seat for 30 minutes while you pay. If the payment is not finished in that time, the seat is released and other people can book it.

## 2. Credits
- One credit books one group class.
- Demo 1:1 package: 5 sessions for €300 (credits valid 16 weeks). [demo price]
- Credits expire on the date shown in "My credits". Expired credits cannot be used. They are not returned and not refunded. [LAWYER: confirm expiry rules]
- Credits belong to your account and cannot be shared or sold.
- Credits from referral rewards follow the same rules. [OWNER: validity]
- Credits have no cash value.

## 3. Cancelling a group class
You can cancel in "My bookings".
- **24 hours or more before the class starts, and you used a credit:** your credit goes back to your account. It keeps its original expiry date. If that credit has already expired, it is not returned.
- **24 hours or more before the class starts, and you paid for a single class by card:** you choose one of two things. Either 1 class credit, valid for 10 weeks, or your money back to your card.
- **Less than 24 hours before the class starts:** you can still cancel, but we do not return the credit and we do not refund the payment.
- You cannot cancel a class that has already started.

## 4. No-shows and late arrival
- If you do not come, the credit or payment is used.
- The "no late join" rule below is for group classes and events. It does **not** apply to 1:1 sessions.
- Please join 5 minutes before the start.
- **Booking closes at the start time of the session.** You can book or register until then. For a session with a greeting (for example Dive Deep and Winter Solstice), the start time is the start of the greeting.
- **Zoom doors close at the start time, too, except for sessions with a greeting.** For those sessions the Zoom doors close when the main activation begins. The session page shows both times (greeting and activation). You can join the greeting, but nobody can join after the activation has started.
- If you arrive after the doors have closed, we cannot let you in, and we do not give a refund or credit for the missed session.
- If you have an emergency or a technical problem, please write to us. We may help at our discretion, but this is not a right.

## 5. 1:1 sessions
- 1:1 sessions follow the same rules as group classes (see section 3): 24 hours or more before the start, a credit goes back, or, if you paid by card, you choose 1 credit (valid 10 weeks) or your money back. Less than 24 hours before the start, you can still cancel, but there is no credit and no refund.
- A credit you get for a cancelled 1:1 session is a 1:1 credit.
- There is no "move" button. To change the time, cancel (under the rules above) and book a new time.
- 1:1 package credits expire 16 weeks after purchase, on the date shown in your account.
- If we need to cancel a 1:1 session, we will contact you. If you used a credit, you get the credit back. If you paid by card, you get your money back.
- The "doors close at the start time, no late join" rule does not apply to 1:1 sessions. If you are late for a 1:1 session, you can still join, but the session ends at the planned time.
- For in-person sessions please arrive on time. Late arrival shortens the session. [OWNER: confirm.]

## 6. Events
- Free events: you can cancel any time before the event starts. Please cancel so others can join.
- Donation events: you can cancel any time before the event starts. A donation is never refunded, also when you cancel before the event. If you chose €0, nothing was paid.
- Paid events: if you cancel 7 days or more before the event starts, you get your money back. If you cancel later, we cannot give the money back. [OWNER: how long a refund takes]
- If we cancel a paid event, you get a full refund of what you paid.
- Donations (on the Donate page or at an event) are voluntary gifts and are never refunded.

## 7. Online sessions: camera, health and recording

## 8. If we cancel or change a class
We will email you as early as we can. If we cancel a class, you get your credit back if you used a credit, or your money back if you paid by card.

This policy does not limit your consumer rights, including the 14-day withdrawal right described in our Terms and Conditions.
