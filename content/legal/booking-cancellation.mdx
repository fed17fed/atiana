# Booking and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

## 1. Booking a group class
- You can pay for one class, or use one credit from a class pack.
- Before you book, you must accept our Health Notice.
- Your seat is confirmed after payment or after the credit is used. We send an email with the Zoom link.
- Classes have limited places. A full class shows "Full".
- We may hold your seat for a short time while you pay. [OWNER: hold time]

## 2. Class packs and credits
- One credit books one group class.
- Demo packs: 5 classes for €60 (valid 10 weeks) and 10 classes for €105 (valid 16 weeks). [demo price]
- Credits expire on the date shown in "My credits". Expired credits cannot be used or refunded. [OWNER/LAWYER: confirm expiry rules]
- Credits belong to your account and cannot be shared or sold.
- Credits from referral rewards follow the same rules. [OWNER: validity]
- Credits have no cash value.

## 3. Cancelling a group class
- **Up to 24 hours before the class:** you can cancel in "My bookings". Your credit goes back to your account. If you paid for a single class, you also get a credit. [OWNER: confirm credit or money back for single classes]
- **Less than 24 hours before:** the booking cannot be cancelled. The credit or payment is used.
- Returned credits keep their original expiry date.

## 4. No-shows and late arrival
- If you do not come, the credit or payment is used.
- If you arrive late, you can join if the class is still open. We cannot give a refund for missed time.
- If you have an emergency or a technical problem, please write to us. We may help at our discretion, but this is not a right.

## 5. 1:1 sessions
- You can move or cancel up to 24 hours before the session. You get your credit back or a credit for the same value. [OWNER: confirm refund or credit]
- Less than 24 hours before, or if you do not come: the session is used.
- If we need to cancel, you can pick a new time or get a full refund.
- For in-person sessions please arrive on time. Late arrival shortens the session. [OWNER: confirm]
- 1:1 package credits (if you buy a package) expire on the date shown in your account. [OWNER: validity]

## 6. Events
- Free events: please cancel in "My bookings" so others can join.
- Paid events: [OWNER: cancellation and refund rule for events, for example 7 days before].

- If we cancel an event, we refund all payments in full.

## 7. If we cancel or change a class
We will email you as early as we can. You can use your credit for another class, or ask for a refund of a single class payment.
