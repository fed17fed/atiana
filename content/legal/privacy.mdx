## 2. What data we collect
- **Account and booking data:** your email, name, bookings, class credits, referral code and referral link use.
- **Order and payment data:** what you bought, amounts and a payment reference. Card details are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
- **Health notice acceptance:** we store the date and time you accepted our Health notice before a booking. We do not ask for health details. Please do not send health details unless needed. If you do, we treat them with care.
- **Messages:** what you write in our contact form or emails.
- **Newsletter data:** your email, the time you subscribed and confirmed (double opt-in).
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser type, and security logs. See our Cookie Policy.
- **Donations:** the email you give (a name is not asked for or saved) and the amount. We send a thank-you email after a donation. To stop misuse of the donation form, we keep a one-way code (a hash) made from your IP address for a short time, never the IP address itself. [LAWYER / OWNER: retention period for this code.]
