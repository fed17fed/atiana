# Health Notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026.

## Please read before you book

KEA by Atiana offers online Kundalini Energy Activation (KEA) sessions and events: you lie down, listen to music and allow what arises. Many people say they feel calmer afterwards. But results are different for each person, and we cannot promise any result.

## Not medical advice
- Our classes, sessions and events are **not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment**.
- They do not replace care from a doctor, nurse, therapist or other health professional.
- Please do not stop or change any medical treatment because of our content.
- If you feel unwell, have pain, or have a health worry, talk to your doctor.

## Please do not join if
- You are prone to mania, delusion or psychosis. KEA is not recommended and is not safe for these conditions.
- You are not sure. Please talk to your doctor first, and write to us before you book.

## Please get your doctor's approval first if
- you are pregnant, or you are post-natal (after giving birth), or you are post-surgical (after an operation);
- you have a heart condition, high or low blood pressure, asthma or breathing problems, epilepsy, or a history of fainting;
- you have a mental health condition, or you are in a period of high stress or trauma;
- you have had a recent injury or illness;
- you are not sure if the practice is right for you.

## Before the session
- Please have a light stomach: 2 hours after a meal, or 30 minutes after a snack.
- You lie down for the whole session. Please choose a safe, quiet place where you will not be disturbed.
- Your camera must be on and your full body must be visible, because the teacher watches each person during the session.
- Tell the teacher about any medical condition or physical limit before the session starts. Use the contact form on this site (page: /contact). Choose a topic about your session and write that you want the teacher to know about a health point. Please do not write more health detail than needed.

## During practice
- You take part by your own choice and you are responsible for your own safety. [LAWYER: the old text said "at your own risk". This is close to liability wording. Lawyer to decide. No waiver text is added.]
- Allow whatever arises. Go at your own pace. Never force your breath or your body.
- If you feel dizzy, short of breath, anxious or in pain, stop at once, return to normal breathing and rest, and ask the teacher for support if you need it. Get medical help if needed.
- Practise in a safe place. Do not practise while driving or near water.
- In an emergency, call 112 or 999.

## Tell your teacher
For all sessions (group classes, events and 1:1), please tell the teacher about anything that may matter for your safety. Do not send health details by email unless needed. We treat them with care. See our Privacy Policy.

## The checkbox before booking
Before you book, we ask you to tick a box: "I have read the Health Notice. I understand that this is not medical advice, and I take part by my own choice." We keep the date and time of your answer. [LAWYER: review wording and liability limits under Irish law.]
